Alice was beginning to get very tired of sitting by her sister on the bank, and of having nothing to do: once or twice she had peeped into the book her sister was reading, but it had no pictures or conversations in it.

So she was considering in her own mind whether the pleasure of making a daisy chain would be worth the trouble of getting up and picking the daisies, when suddenly a White Rabbit with pink eyes ran close by her.

The Time Traveller had finished the machine and rested his hand upon the brass rail. The model glittered under the lamp. It was a strange device, with bars, dials, and a saddle arranged in a form that was both delicate and severe.

He pressed the lever and the laboratory whirled. Day and night chased one another, and the city beyond the windows changed as seasons leaped and vanished.
